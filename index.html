01 / THE UZZAP STORY
Rooms before feeds.

UZZAP is presented here through its community surfaces: Philippine rooms, province-based discovery, buddies, private conversations and the little interface details that made the experience feel like its own place.

The point is atmosphere — the feeling of entering a room, seeing who is around, and staying for the conversation.
