★ QUESTIONS ★

BEFORE YOU ASK.

Quick answers. Still stuck? Open an issue on the UzzapAndroid repo.

Q>Is Uzzap free?

Yes. Free to download, free to chat. No carrier fees — that era is over.

Q>What Android version do I need?

Android 9.0 or newer, plus an internet connection. Download the APK, allow Install from unknown sources once, and register in the app.

Q>Is this the original Uzzap?

No. This is an independent, unofficial recreation of the classic Nokia/J2ME Uzzap experience — rebuilt from scratch in Kotlin. Not affiliated with the original owners.

Q>Where is my data stored?

On Uzzap's secure cloud backend. Your account survives reinstalls — just sign back in. See the privacy policy.

Q>How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Privacy → Request Account Deletion. Without the app: use the web deletion page.

Q>Which rooms exist?

83 province rooms across 18 Philippine regions, plus a moderator room. Browse them all on the rooms page.

Q>The app says Beta — what breaks?

Occasionally: features may glitch and accounts may be reset during the beta. See the changelog for what's new and what's fixed.

Q>Someone is harassing me. What do I do?

Block them (chat options → Block User) and file a report (Report User). Moderators review every report and can warn, mute, kick or ban.
