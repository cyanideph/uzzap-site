DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, buddy list and message history. This cannot be undone. Limited records may be retained for security, fraud-prevention or legal reasons — see the privacy policy.

OPTION 1 — INSIDE THE APP (FASTEST)

  1. Open Uzzap and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Request Account Deletion.
  3. Confirm. You'll be signed out immediately.

OPTION 2 — WEB REQUEST (NO APP NEEDED)

Uninstalled the app or can't sign in? Enter the email address on your account below. We record the request and complete deletion on the backend.
